Admissions 2026–27

Five steps, about
three weeks, no capitation

Seats are open for Nursery, LKG, UKG, and Grades 1 to 5. Nursery and Grade 1 fill first, usually by January. Everything below happens on campus — transparent, caring, and merit-based.

How admission works

1
Enquiry

Fill the form here or call the office. We reply the same working day with available seats for your child's grade.

2
Application

Collect the form at reception or download it. Application fee is ₹500, payable online or at the counter.

3
Interaction

A relaxed thirty-minute playful interaction. Nursery to UKG meet with our early-years educators; Grades 1 to 5 have a friendly reading and basic numeracy session.

4
Document verification

Originals checked at the office while you wait. Nothing is retained except copies.

5
Confirmation

Offer sent within five working days. Seat is held for one week from the offer date.

What to bring

  • Birth certificate (original + copy)
  • Transfer certificate from previous school (Grades 1-5)
  • Previous progress report (if applicable)
  • Aadhaar of child and both parents
  • Four passport photographs
  • Address proof and immunisation record
