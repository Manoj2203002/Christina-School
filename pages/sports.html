Sports & Athletics

Fostering teamwork, discipline, and physical fitness.

Our Sporting Spirit

At Christina Nursery & Primary School, we believe that a healthy mind resides in a healthy body. We provide excellent facilities and coaching for traditional Indian sports as well as modern athletic events.

Our students regularly participate and win laurels in District and State-level competitions across Tamil Nadu.

Sports Offerings

Cricket

Professional coaching net sessions and intra-school tournaments.

Kabaddi & Kho-Kho

Embracing native Indian sports that build immense stamina and agility.

Athletics

Track and field events: sprinting, long jump, and relay races.

Badminton & TT

Indoor sports to enhance hand-eye coordination and reflexes.
