About our school

Small enough to know
every single child

Christina Nursery and Primary School began with a warm promise in Anna Nagar West: every teacher knows every child in their corridor by name, and knows what makes each of them light up. We nurture children through their formative years from Nursery, LKG, UKG up to Grade 5.

Class sizes are strictly capped at 25. Homework is purposeful and never burdensome. We believe in joyful learning that builds character and confidence.

Academic stages

Nursery to Grade 5: A joyful journey of discovery

Nursery, LKG & UKG

Early Years: Joyful Foundation

Sensory exploration, phonics, number sense, and social development. Children learn through guided play, music, storytelling, and creative motor activities in a safe, cheerful setting.

  • Caring early childhood educators with 1:12 ratio
  • Sensory tables, building blocks & picture library
  • Daily music, movement, and outdoor play
Grades 1 to 5

Primary School: Building Strong Minds

Conceptual mathematics, bilingual literacy, environmental science, and computer curiosity. Students learn to question, write expressively, and collaborate joyfully.

  • Class size capped at 25 with individual attention
  • Junior science experiments & digital literacy
  • Creative arts, sports, and value education

Why Christina

Four things parents value most

Teachers who stay

Nineteen of our dedicated teachers have taught at Christina for many years. Your child is mentored by educators who genuinely care and know them deeply.

An hour outdoors, every day

Games and athletics are timetabled every single day. Six sports run year-round and every child represents their house team.

Labs built for doing, not display

From Grade 3, every child puts on a lab apron. Physics and biology concepts are tested on the bench before they are written in notebooks.

Safe, caring campus

Gated, staffed, and covered by monitored safety systems. A full-time nurse, clear anti-bullying protocols, and caring corridor mentors.
