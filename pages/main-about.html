Our school

Small enough to know
every single child

Christina Nursery and Primary School began with a clear purpose: nurturing young children from Nursery to Grade 5 with loving care, curiosity, and strong foundational learning. We have grown while keeping the one thing that matters most: every teacher knows every child by name, and knows what makes each of them light up.

Class sizes are capped at 25. Homework is capped too. We would rather a child read and explore for an hour than copy for two.

A school where children graduate from Grade 5 able to think clearly, speak kindly, read voraciously, and teach themselves something new with confidence.

Teach foundational concepts thoroughly. Keep classes small. Give every child a stage, a creative corner and an outdoor play area, not just a desk.

Curiosity before marks. Honesty and moral values. Kindness to the youngest child in the corridor. Everyday effort that shapes bright futures.

Why families choose us

Six things parents tell us
made the difference

Not a list of promises. These are the reasons that came up most often in last year's parent survey, in their words and ours.

Teachers who stay

Nineteen of our dedicated teachers have been at Christina for years. Your child's early childhood and primary teachers work hand-in-hand with parents to ensure every milestone is celebrated.

An hour outdoors, every single day

Games, athletics and free play are timetabled, not squeezed in. Sports run through the year and every child in Grades 1 to 5 represents a house team.

Academics with room to breathe

Concepts are taught to mastery before the next one begins. Reading, writing and number sense are assessed every term, and support classes are free.

Technology where it earns its place

Interactive displays in classrooms and a child-friendly computer lab. Screen-free sensory play in Nursery & Kindergarten — they are busy exploring and learning to read.

Character, practised out loud

Weekly circle time, a caring buddy system pairing Grade 5 with Nursery/LKG, and junior student leaders who help lead assemblies.

A campus you can stop worrying about

Single gated entry, CCTV across corridors and grounds, verified staff, a full-time nurse, and GPS-tracked buses with a female attendant on every route.
