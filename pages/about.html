About Us

Our Founding Story

Established in 1992 in the vibrant town of Attur, Salem, Christina Nursery & Primary School was founded with a singular vision: to provide high-quality, holistic education to children in our community. Rooted in traditional values yet progressive in our methodology, we align with the Tamil Nadu State Board curriculum to ensure our students excel academically and personally.


Over three decades, we have grown from a small nursery into a respected institution offering Pre-KG to 8th Standard education, nurturing thousands of bright minds.

Principal's Message

"Welcome to Christina Nursery & Primary School. Education is the most powerful tool we can use to change the world. Here, we strive to build a strong foundation for our children, instilling moral values, critical thinking, and a lifelong love for learning. We embrace the rich culture of Tamil Nadu while preparing our students for a global future."


— Mrs. Lakshmi Narayanan, Principal

Our Journey (1992–2026)

1992

Founded as a humble nursery school in Gopalapuram, Attur with 30 students.

2005

Upgraded to a Primary School recognized by the Tamil Nadu State Government.

2018

Expanded to offer Middle School education up to 8th Standard with modern facilities.
