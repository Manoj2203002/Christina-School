Admissions

Join our community for the April to March Academic Year.

Admission Process

1. Enquiry

Submit an online enquiry or contact our office via phone or WhatsApp.

2. Campus Visit

Tour the campus and meet with our academic counselors in Attur.

3. Registration

Fill out the registration form and submit required documents.

4. Enrollment

Pay the admission fee to confirm your child's seat.

Required Documents

  • Copy of Birth Certificate
  • Copy of student's Aadhaar Card
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous school (if applicable)
  • 4 Passport size photographs of the student
  • Address proof of parents
