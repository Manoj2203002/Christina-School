Academics

Tamil Nadu State Board Curriculum (Pre-KG to 8th Standard)

Kindergarten

Pre-KG, LKG, UKG

A playful and nurturing environment introducing early literacy, numeracy, Tamil rhymes, and interactive learning.

Primary Section

1st to 5th Standard

Building a strong foundation in Tamil, English, Mathematics, Science, and Social Science through engaging Samacheer Kalvi syllabus.

Middle School

6th to 8th Standard

Fostering critical thinking and practical knowledge with detailed studies in all subjects, including Computer Science and communicative English.

Our Core Subjects

Tamil (தமிழ்)

Emphasizing reading, writing, and literature of our rich state language.

English

Focusing on grammar, spoken English, and global communication skills.

Mathematics

Mental math, logical reasoning, and problem-solving techniques.

Science

Practical experiments and theoretical knowledge in basic sciences.

Social Science

Indian history, geography, and civic responsibilities.

Computer Science

Basic IT skills, coding fundamentals, and digital literacy.
