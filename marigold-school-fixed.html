Our school

Small enough to know
every single child

Marigold began in 2004 in a rented hall with nineteen children and four teachers. We have grown to 412 children across Grades 1 to 8, and we have kept the one thing that made those first years work: every teacher knows every child in their corridor by name, and knows what makes each of them light up.

Class sizes are capped at 28. Homework is capped too. We would rather a child read for an hour than copy for two.

A school where children leave Grade 8 able to think clearly, argue kindly, and teach themselves something new without being told to.

Teach fewer things properly. Keep classes small. Give every child a stage, a field and a lab, not just a desk. Talk to parents before there is a problem, not after.

Curiosity before marks. Honesty even when it costs. Kindness to the youngest child in the corridor. Effort that shows up on ordinary Tuesdays, not only on prize day.

Why families choose us

Six things parents tell us
made the difference

Not a list of promises. These are the reasons that came up most often in last year's parent survey, in their words and ours.

Teachers who stay

Nineteen of our thirty-four teachers have been at Marigold for more than five years. Your child's Grade 4 teacher will still be in the corridor to ask after them in Grade 7. Every child also has a mentor teacher who meets their parents twice a term.

An hour outdoors, every single day

Games, athletics and free play are timetabled, not squeezed in. Six sports run through the year and every child in Grades 3 to 8 represents a house team.

Academics with room to breathe

Concepts are taught to mastery before the next one begins. Reading, writing and number sense are assessed every term, and support classes are free.

Technology where it earns its place

Smart boards in every classroom and a 40-seat computer lab. No screens in Grades 1 and 2 beyond the teacher's board — they are busy learning to read.

Character, practised out loud

Weekly circle time, a buddy system pairing Grade 7 with Grade 1, and a student council that actually gets a budget.

A campus you can stop worrying about

Single gated entry, CCTV across corridors and grounds, verified staff, a full-time nurse, and GPS-tracked buses with a female attendant on every route.

Admissions 2026–27

Five steps, about
three weeks, no capitation

Seats are open in Grades 1 to 8. Grade 1 fills first, usually by January. Everything below happens on campus — there is no agent and no donation.

How admission works

1
Enquiry

Fill the form here or call the office. We reply the same working day with available seats for your child's grade.

2
Application

Collect the form at reception or download it. Application fee is ₹500, payable online or at the counter.

3
Interaction

A relaxed forty-minute session. Grades 1 and 2 meet a teacher and play; Grades 3 to 8 write a short language and maths assessment.

4
Document verification

Originals checked at the office while you wait. Nothing is retained except copies.

5
Confirmation

Offer sent within five working days. Seat is held for one week from the offer date.

What to bring

  • Birth certificate (original + copy)
  • Transfer certificate from previous school
  • Last two report cards
  • Aadhaar of child and both parents
  • Four passport photographs
  • Address proof and blood group record
